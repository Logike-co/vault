
1. Product design documents. These describe the user problem being solved, and how that problem is solved from a user's point of view. Wireframes are frequently used in cases where a potential UI needs to be described, but they are meant to convey product ideas, not be anything close to a spec.
2. Engineering design documents. These describe the engineering problem being solved and go into enough detail on the major or complex parts of the solution so that a typical senior engineer could understand the design decisions and why they were made the way they were.
3. Mocks. Once a product is under active development, these are visual mockups of the UI that designers create to show exactly what it should look like.
